Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are a simple breakfast dish made from a batter of flour, milk, eggs, and other basic ingredients, cooked until golden brown.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Butter
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Whisk the milk and eggs together in a separate bowl.
  3. Combine the wet and dry ingredients until just mixed.
  4. Heat a lightly buttered pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour some batter into the pan and cook until bubbles form.
  6. Flip the pancake and cook the other side until golden brown.
  7. Repeat with the remaining batter and serve.
