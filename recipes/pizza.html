Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a baked dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of toppings.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Pepperoni
  • Bell pepper
  • Onion
  • Olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough.
  3. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  4. Add mozzarella cheese and your desired toppings.
  5. Drizzle a little olive oil over the pizza.
  6. Bake until the crust is golden and the cheese is melted.
  7. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes before serving.
